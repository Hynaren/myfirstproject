# Daily Quest #3 — Open free Mall Chests

## Goal

Complete the Daily Quest by opening one currently free Mall chest.
The quest target is 1 chest.

## Confirmed LDPlayer flow

The supplied live screenshots establish this navigation:

1. From Castle, tap the **Shop shortcut beside the Solo shortcut**.
2. The Shop shortcut uses the same fixed event-slot Y position as Solo.
   Current 960x540 baseline: approximately (355, 67).
3. Swipe **up on the left category rail** to bring **Special Bundles** into view.
4. Detect and tap **Special Bundles**.
5. Detect and tap **Best Sellers**.
6. Swipe **up on the right Best Sellers content area** to scroll the offers down.
7. Detect the **Free** chest.
8. Tap the detected chest.
9. Re-scan the active Free-chest visual. Quest succeeds only when that
   active/free visual is no longer present.

## Architecture

AutomationEngine
→ Global Popup Safety
→ QuestManager
→ OpenFreeMallChestsRoutine
→ ActionEngine
→ Detector / Vision
→ ADB

The routine does not talk directly to ADB.

## Vision assets

Required production templates:

- assets/mall_chests/special_bundles.png
  - Special Bundles entry in the left category rail.
- assets/mall_chests/best_sellers.png
  - Best Sellers entry after Special Bundles is expanded.
- assets/mall_chests/free_chest.png
  - The active Free chest visual used for both target detection and
    post-claim verification.

The Shop shortcut itself is intentionally **not** template-matched because
its artwork changes with the active event/shortcut. Its fixed shortcut slot
is the navigation contract.

## Scroll strategy

Left rail:
- bounded upward swipe from (120, 420) to (120, 180).

Right Best Sellers content:
- bounded upward finger swipe from (820, 440) to (820, 180).
- routine permits up to two content scrolls and scans before each scroll.

No chest coordinate is hardcoded.

## Safety

- Missing production templates fail before any game interaction.
- Global popup handling remains owned by AutomationEngine / PopupManager.
- The routine does not duplicate popup handling.
- If the Free chest remains active after the claim tap, the quest fails
  instead of assuming completion.
- No OCR, web screenshot, or invented chest coordinate is used.
